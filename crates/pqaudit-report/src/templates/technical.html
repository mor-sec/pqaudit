Method

The tool scanned files and network endpoints for cryptography. It compared each item with the rules pack. It then added the context of the customer from the assets file.

Each finding has a score from 0 to 100. The score is the weighted sum of six factors: threat, exposure, sensitivity, mosca, deadline, and scale. A field that the customer did not set has the cautious value. The report shows such a field as Unassessed.

The suggested band comes from the score. A customer can override the band. The report shows the original band, the override, and the reason.
